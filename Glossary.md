# Glossary

## Core terms

- Recipe: A single note that captures one meal or dish, including ingredients, steps, servings, and prep notes.
- Shopping list: A living list of items needed for the meals you plan to cook.
- Meal prep: Preparing food in advance so you can reuse it across multiple days.
- Pantry staple: A food item you usually keep in stock and reuse across many recipes.
- Batch cook: Making a larger quantity of one meal so it can be eaten over several days.
- Prep day: A scheduled day for chopping, cooking, or portioning food in advance.
- Rotation: A small set of reliable meals you return to regularly.
- Ingredient: A single item in a recipe or shopping list.
- Serving size: How many people or meals a recipe is intended to cover.

## Useful tags

- #recipe: A note describing a recipe.
- #shopping-list: A note used for current or future shopping needs.
- #meal-prep: A recipe or note meant for batch cooking or prep planning.
- #vegetarian, #high-protein, #budget, #quick: Helpful tags for filtering.
- #weekly-plan: A note for planning meals for the week.

## Recommended note types

- Recipe note: Contains ingredients, steps, timing, and storage notes.
- Shopping list note: Contains grouped sections such as produce, protein, dairy, pantry, and frozen goods.
- Weekly plan note: Keeps track of meals you intend to cook this week.

## Suggested metadata fields

Use these fields in recipe notes to make them easier to search and filter:

- Source
- Servings
- Prep time
- Cook time
- Difficulty
- Diet
- Storage life

## Best-practice rule of thumb

Keep each recipe as one note, keep the shopping list separate, and link them together with tags or backlinks rather than duplicating information.
